Modes

🐉 Co-DM

A second DM: every DM control, on their own device. Don't show it on the TV.

⚔️ Battle Master

Runs combat for you: initiative and turns, the battle map and fog, and your creatures' attacks, HP and conditions. Nothing outside combat. Don't show it on the TV.

💬 Social Master

Voices NPCs for you: types their lines into the dialogue box, sets emotions, picks who is on the scene and pushes player choices. Nothing else. Don't show it on the TV.

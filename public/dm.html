🐉 Co-DM

A second DM: every DM control, on their own device. Don't show it on the TV.

⚔️ Battle Master

Runs combat for you: initiative and turns, the battle map and fog, and your creatures' attacks, HP and conditions. Nothing outside combat. Don't show it on the TV.

💬 Social Master

Voices NPCs for you: types their lines into the dialogue box, sets emotions and picks who is on the scene. Nothing else. Don't show it on the TV.

🎻 Music Co-DM

Runs the soundscape for you: mood wheel, environment, weather, layers, intensity and favourites. Not the master volume or the output, and you can lock or undo anything they do. Don't show it on the TV.
